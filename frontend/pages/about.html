About Talentum

Talentum is an AI-powered career mentor built to help students and early-career professionals improve their CVs, prepare for interviews, and make smarter career decisions.

What it does

  • CV feedback with clear, actionable improvements
  • Career guidance and next-step planning
  • Writing help (LinkedIn, outreach messages, cover letters)

How it works

Built as a capstone project using FastAPI for the backend and Jinja/HTMX for a fast, lightweight UI. The goal is simple: reduce career stress and increase your chances of landing a role you actually want.

Privacy

Your data stays tied to your account. If you use Guest Mode, your chat won’t be saved. Uploads and personal details are only used to generate your results.
